Guide

How the deep dive works, step by step.

What you do, what you see and what you leave with. You do not have to finish in one sitting: a reading is saved as you go, and you can open it again from History.

Before you start

  • An invitation. Access is by invitation only. There is no sign-up page. We send you a single-use link that works for 7 days.
  • Your company's website. The reading begins from what you publish.
  • A second person to review and sign. A reading is signed by a named reviewer, not by the person who set it up. Bring a colleague, an adviser or an outside reviewer. You invite them from the Review screen; they get their own one-time link and their own password. The signed reading shows who signed it.
  • Documents you are content to share. Policies, contracts, records. Redact personal data first. Public information is enough to begin.

The steps

  1. Accept the invitation and choose a password

    Open your link and set a password of at least 12 characters. The link works once; if it has expired, ask us for another. After that you sign in with your email and that password.

    You will see: "Choose a password", then the start screen.

  2. Say which company, and how you are running the reading

    Your company name and website are filled in for you. Company number and country of registration are optional, and are used as you give them. Tick the box to confirm you are authorised to run a reading for this organisation.

    Then choose Run diagnostic.

    Ovalfort checks robots.txt before it reads any page, and reads public pages only.

  3. Wait for the scan

    The screen lists each stage as it happens: reading the pages and linked documents, storing them as evidence, checking which laws apply, and looking for AI systems. It takes a few minutes. If it cannot run, it says why and nothing is guessed.

  4. Read the first look

    This is what the site tells us, before we ask you anything. It shows which laws are in play and why, the AI systems it found (each only a candidate, picked out by wording), and what your public pages say about AI. If two of your own statements do not sit together, it shows both and does not decide which is right.

    Untick a system we got wrong. Add one we missed. You can download the first look as a note.

    Then choose Confirm and continue.

  5. Answer what applies, most decisive first

    The questions are grouped into areas and ranked by how many checks use the answer. Open an area and answer. "Not sure" is allowed. It is recorded as not sure, which is not the same as established, and for a few questions it counts as not met, and the finding says so.

    A counter shows how many questions are established out of how many apply now. That number can grow when an answer or a document shows something new. It measures what is established. It does not measure compliance. You can return to any area and change an answer.

  6. Add your own evidence (optional, and the part that matters most)

    The screen lists the documents that would settle some of the checks. Add a PDF, a Word document (.docx), plain text or Markdown, up to 25 MB. If the document carries a date, enter it: some checks accept only a recent document, and without a date currency cannot be established.

    Where you have nothing, the reading says so. That is useful to know.

  7. Generate the reading

    You can generate a draft at any point. Questions left open show as not assessed or not enough evidence. If you have answered nothing yet, the button says Generate laws-only reading, and that is exactly what you get: which laws apply, and no findings.

  8. Go through the findings

    Each finding names the law it rests on and quotes the opening of the cited article, with a link to the official source. It shows the evidence it found (quoted, with the document it came from), why it has the state it has, and what would change it. A document that only matches a check's wording is shown as "relevant evidence read, not confirmed", and where the match rests on one keyword of several, the finding is flagged so the reviewer looks at it.

    Decide each finding: Accept, Dispute or Override. Dispute and Override need a reason. Your decision, your account and the time are recorded.

  9. Sign

    When every finding has been decided, Sign becomes available to a reviewer. The signature is the signed-in reviewer's: their email address and the time. It does not say your company is compliant. If you have no reviewer yet, the owner invites one from this screen: choose Create invitation link (shown once; pass it on by a channel you trust). An owner cannot sign their own organisation's reading unless we have agreed an exception with you, and the reading then says so.

  10. Download and keep

    Download the reading as a web page and the findings as a spreadsheet (CSV). A signed reading is frozen: it shows a fingerprint and will not change. To take a fresh reading later, choose Run again from History, and use Compare to see what moved up, moved down or is new.

The 30 days

  • Start. The 30 days run from the date we confirm your acceptance by email, as the beta terms say. Once your workspace has an end date, a notice bar at the top of the screen says how many days are left.
  • After the end date. For 14 days you can still sign in, view and download everything. Nothing new can be added, and the screen says so.
  • Then. Sign-in closes. The software deletes nothing by itself. We delete your workspace within 30 days after the beta ends unless you ask us in writing to keep it longer. Ask us at any time and we delete it straight away; 14 days from your written request is the longest the terms allow. Download what you want to keep first.
  • Want longer? Ask us. The terms let us extend the 30 days if both of us agree in writing, and an extension restores the ability to add.

What you will not find here

A preliminary view of likely obligations and gaps, from public information and what you tell us. It is not legal advice and it is not a finding that any company complies with any law. It does not certify or approve anyone. The India part has not yet had an Indian legal review, and the terms have not yet been reviewed by a solicitor. Ovalfort is a one-person company in stealth; a legal review of both is planned for after we raise funding, and has not started. We do not cover the United States. We never ask for source code, production databases, personal health data, passwords or keys.

If something goes wrong

  • The link says it is no longer valid. It was used, or 7 days passed. Ask for a new one.
  • The scan did not run. The screen gives the reason. Check that the website address is right and public, then run it again.
  • A finding looks wrong. Dispute it with a reason. The reason goes into the record.
  • You want everything deleted. Email us. We delete your workspace straight away.
