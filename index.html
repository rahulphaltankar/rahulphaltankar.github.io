About

I started in testing and business intelligence migration, then moved into running programmes: first as a scrum master and agile programme manager, then as a transformation lead. Most of that work was for banks and insurers, where a mistake in how data is separated, moved or described becomes a regulatory problem.

A company that sells AI into Europe, the UK or India is asked "are you compliant?" long before it can pay for a lawyer. Most answer from a template. I wanted an answer that shows the line of law behind every statement, and says plainly when it cannot tell.

What I'm building

A product that reads a company's public website, asks it a short set of questions, and writes a preliminary reading across the EU AI Act, EU GDPR, UK GDPR and India's data-protection and cyber-incident rules. Every legal statement links to a quotation in the official text, and code checks that the quotation exists. No AI model decides the answer: code reads the evidence against a stored copy of the law.

It is live, and it is early. India has not yet had a legal review, and I do not claim that any company is compliant.
