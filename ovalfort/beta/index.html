The agreement

This is the Ovalfort 30-day beta agreement, version 1.0. It is between you (the company named in the form below, and the person who ticks the boxes for it) and Ovalfort. Ovalfort is an early-stage project run by Rahul Phaltankar, an individual, in the United Kingdom. It is not yet a registered company. If the project moves into a company, we will tell you in writing first, and you may end the beta at that point.

1. What the beta is

For 30 days from the date we confirm your acceptance by email, you may use a private workspace to run a deep dive on your company: answer guided questions, add your own evidence, and receive a written reading and a fix list. The beta is free. It is early software. Features may change, and the service may be slow or unavailable at times. We may extend the 30 days if both of us agree in writing.

2. What you give us, and what you do not

We work from public information about your company and from what you choose to provide. You confirm that you have the right to provide it.

  • Please give us policies, contracts and records, redacted where you can.
  • Please do not give us source code, production databases, personal health data, personal data about identifiable people, or passwords, keys and tokens. If something like this arrives by mistake, tell us and we will delete it.
  • We read public web pages only, and we respect robots.txt.

3. Confidential information

Confidential information means any non-public information one of us gives the other for the beta. From you, that includes your answers, your uploaded documents and the reading about your company. From us, it includes unpublished product details, prompts, features and pricing.

Each of us will use the other's confidential information only for the beta, protect it with at least reasonable care, and share it only with people who need it and are bound to keep it confidential. We may share your information with the service providers named in section 4, and only as needed to run the beta.

This does not cover information that is or becomes public through no fault of the receiver, was already known to the receiver, was developed independently, or was lawfully received from someone else. If the law or a regulator requires disclosure, the receiver will tell the other party first where the law allows. This duty lasts for the beta and for 3 years after it ends.

We will not name you, your company or your reading in public, in marketing or to other prospects without your written permission. You may give us feedback, and we may use it to improve the product without naming you. You will not copy the product or try to take it apart to learn how it works.

4. Your data

We store your answers, your uploaded evidence, the public pages we read, your readings and a record of how each reading was reached. They are held in a private workspace in Google Cloud's London region, separate from every other company's workspace.

To draft wording, we send the text needed for your reading to an AI model provider. The providers we may use are Google (Gemini) and Mistral AI, under their standard business terms. We will tell you if that list changes. Citations are checked by our own code, not by the model.

On your written request at any time, we will delete your data within 14 days, except what the law requires us to keep. After the beta ends, we will delete your answers and uploaded evidence within 30 days unless you ask us in writing to keep them longer. Before then, you can download your reading and fix list.

If we learn that someone has gained access to your data without permission, we will tell you without undue delay and in any case within 72 hours. We use your team's contact details only to run the beta.

5. What the reading is, and is not

The reading is a preliminary view of likely obligations and gaps. It is not legal advice. It is not a finding that your company does or does not comply with any law, and it does not certify or approve anyone. The India part has not yet been reviewed by an Indian lawyer. You stay responsible for your own decisions, and you should take legal advice before acting on a finding.

6. No warranty, and limits on liability

The beta is provided as it is, with no warranty. To the extent the law allows, we are not liable for indirect loss, loss of profit or loss of business, and our total liability to you for anything connected to the beta is limited to 100 pounds. Nothing in this agreement limits liability that cannot be limited by law, including for fraud, or for death or personal injury caused by negligence.

7. Ending the beta

Either of us may end the beta at any time by email. It also ends on its own 30 days after we confirm your acceptance, unless extended in writing. Sections 3, 4, 5 and 6 continue after it ends.

8. How you accept, and the governing law

Ticking the boxes below and sending the email is your offer to accept these terms. The agreement is made, and the 30 days begin, when we confirm by email. Nothing is recorded on this website itself. The email you send and our reply are the record.
